WELCOME TO THUNDER STUDIO V1.1!
The industry's first autonomous AI highlight automation studio tailored for
competitive PUBG Mobile creators, esports athletes, and content producers.

WHAT'S NEW IN V1.1:
--------------------
* Turbo vs Normal Background CPU Mode (Zero FPS Drop during Gameloop matches)
* Frame-Accurate Scrubbing: Draggable Playhead on Timeline Clips
* Seamless Trimming: Auto-pause during trim with anti-skip clip guard
* Multi-Part Highlight Export & Organizer
* Official Discord Community & Instant Error Reporting

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
--------------------
* Operating System: 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11
* Processor: Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or higher
* Memory: 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended for 60fps renders)
* Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1050 / AMD Radeon RX 560 or equivalent GPU
* Storage: 500 MB free space for installation (+ disk space for video outputs)
* Video Source: 1080p 60fps or 30fps MP4 gameplay recordings from OBS, GameLoop,
  or standard screen recorders.

QUICK START IN 3 EASY STEPS:
----------------------------
1. Launch THUNDER Studio from your Desktop or Start Menu.
2. If this is your first time, enter your Pakistani Pass or International License Key.
3. Select your raw match capture folder, choose your music track, and click
   "GENERATE AI HIGHLIGHTS" — THUNDER will automatically isolate knocks, eliminations,
   and squad wipes into ready-to-publish esports montages and vertical shorts!
