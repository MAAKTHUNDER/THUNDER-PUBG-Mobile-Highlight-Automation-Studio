FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does the license key activation work?

Upon purchasing or claiming a trial, your license key is displayed on screen and sent to your email. Inside THUNDER Studio Desktop, click "Plan Setting", paste your key, and click "Activate Key". It cryptographically locks to your PC hardware.

Can I switch my license to a new PC?

Yes. Inside the desktop app, click "Remove / Reset License Key" to unlink your hardware. You can then activate the key on your new computer.

What happens when my 15-Day Free Trial ends?
